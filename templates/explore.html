Image Upload Instructions for Accurate Grading
  • Use Good Lighting: Take photos in bright, natural light without shadows
  • Proper Distance: Keep the camera 20-30 cm away from the fruit
  • Focus on Fruit: Ensure the fruit is centered and in clear focus
  • Plain Background: Use a solid, contrasting background (white or black works best)
  • Multiple Angles: Capture different sides of the fruit for comprehensive analysis
  • Avoid: Blurry images, poor lighting, hands in frame, or multiple fruits
