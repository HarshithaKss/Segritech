Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our agricultural technology solutions

How does SegriTech's seed sorting technology work?

Our advanced AI-powered seed sorting machines use computer vision and machine learning algorithms to analyze each seed individually. The system can detect size, shape, color, density, and quality parameters to ensure only the highest grade seeds pass through, reducing post-harvest losses by up to 40%.

What crops can your machines process?

Our technology supports a wide range of crops including wheat, rice, maize, soybeans, pulses, sunflower seeds, and many specialty crops. Each machine can be calibrated for specific crop types and varieties to ensure optimal sorting accuracy.

How long is the warranty and what support do you provide?

We provide a comprehensive 2-year warranty on all equipment with 24/7 technical support. Our service includes on-site installation, training, regular maintenance, and remote monitoring capabilities. Extended warranty options are also available.

Can I see a demonstration of the technology?

Absolutely! We offer live demonstrations at our Innovation Center in Bangalore, virtual demos via video call, and can arrange on-site demonstrations at your facility. Contact us to schedule a demo that works best for you.

What financing options are available?

We offer flexible financing solutions including equipment leasing, installment plans, and partnerships with agricultural banks. Government subsidies for agricultural technology may also be available. Our finance team can help you find the best option for your needs.
