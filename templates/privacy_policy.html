Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us at namaste@segritech.com. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on our website.

Data Deletion

We respect your right to privacy and your right to request the deletion of your personal data. To request data deletion, please send an email to namaste@segritech.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request," including your full name, email address, and a detailed description of the data you want to be deleted. To protect your privacy and security, we may take reasonable steps to verify your identity before processing your request.

Upon receiving your request, we will:

  • Acknowledge receipt within 7 working days
  • Verify your identity and the validity of your request
  • Delete your personal data from our records unless retention is required or permitted by law
  • Notify you of the completion of your request

There may be circumstances where we cannot comply with your data deletion request, such as compliance with legal obligations, resolving disputes, or enforcing our agreements. In such cases, we will inform you of the reason for the refusal.

If you have any questions about our Data Deletion Policy, please contact us at namaste@segritech.com.
